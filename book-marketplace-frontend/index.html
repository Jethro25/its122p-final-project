COMMUNITY STANDARDS

Built on Accountability

Why readers choose Librowse over unmoderated social media threads.

Honest Condition Grading

No misleading photos. Every listing is cataloged with strict condition definitions (New, Good, Acceptable) so buyers know exact cover and page wear.

Dedicated Staff Mediation

If an order goes sideways or condition doesn't match the listing, our student staff review dispute reports and process refunds directly through the support portal.

Sustainable Campus Exchange

Extend the lifecycle of paperbacks, hardcovers, and required course textbooks. Keep good literature in circulation while keeping costs accessible.
